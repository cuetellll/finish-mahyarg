FAQ

Things you will probably ask

Who is Mahyar Gap for?
Small private groups — a handful of friends who want their own space that is light, fast and free of ads.
How does the music bot work?
“Mahyar Music” joins the LiveKit voice room as a real participant and publishes the same audio track to everyone. Each listener adjusts their own volume or mutes the music locally.
Where does the music come from?
Only from that server's shared library: files your members upload themselves. Mahyar Gap does not scrape or stream Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube.
How do I create an account?
Press “Get started”, choose a username and email, and sign in. To join a friend's server, open their invite link.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. This page and the app are responsive for phones, tablets and desktop. On touch devices the icon pointer effect is disabled to save resources.
Why will my camera not start?
Usually the camera permission was denied or another app is using the camera. The error message names the exact cause, and your microphone and voice connection stay connected either way.

It takes a few minutes: create an account, invite your friends and play the first shared track.
